Frequently asked questions

Notifications don't arrive

  • Check that notifications are allowed in Settings → Notifications → ThenNext on your iPhone.
  • If they aren't, the app shows "Notifications are off" on the list screen, with a button that opens Settings.
  • Also check that the reminder is turned on for that item.

Restoring a purchase

In the app, go to Settings → Plan → Restore purchase. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.

Moving to a new iPhone

In the app, choose Settings → Export JSON and save the file, then choose Import JSON on the new device. Both are free to use. Importing replaces the data already on that device.

Is there a limit on reminders?

Up to 60 items can have a reminder turned on. With Pro, there is no limit on how many items you can add.

Where is my data stored?

Only on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any other server. See the Privacy Policy for details.
