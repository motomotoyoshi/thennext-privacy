Privacy Policy

App: ThenNext (アレハイツ)

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Summary

ThenNext does not collect any personal information.

All data the app handles stays on your device. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third-party server. No account is required.

1. Information We Collect

None.

The app does not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, date of birth, location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.

There is no analytics, advertising, or tracking of any kind. No third-party SDKs are included.

2. Information Stored on Your Device

The following information you enter in the app is stored only on your device:

  • Item titles, notes, and categories
  • History of completion dates
  • Reminder settings (interval and time of day)
  • App settings (sort order, default notification time)

This data is kept in the app's own storage and in an App Group container (a device-local area shared only between the app and its widget). It is never transmitted anywhere.

Deleting the app removes this data from your device.

3. Notifications

All reminders are local notifications. They are scheduled and delivered entirely on your device, and their contents never pass through any server. The app does not use push (remote) notifications.

You can change notification permission at any time in the Settings app. If notifications are not allowed, your reminder settings are preserved but notifications will not be delivered.

4. In-App Purchase (Pro)

Purchases are processed through Apple's systems (App Store / StoreKit).

  • Payment details, such as credit card numbers, are never passed to the app. The developer does not receive or store them.
  • The app receives only whether a purchase is active, and uses it to unlock features.
  • Data handling during the purchase is governed by Apple's privacy policy.

5. Backup (Export and Restore)

You can export your data as a JSON file.

  • You choose where the exported file is saved (on your device, the Files app, iCloud Drive, and so on).
  • The developer never receives this file.
  • If you share the exported file with others, its handling becomes your responsibility.

6. Diagnostic Information

The app may write operational logs on your device. These logs remain on the device and are not sent to the developer automatically.

If the app terminates unexpectedly, Apple's system may generate a crash report. Whether that report is shared with the developer is controlled by you in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements. Crash reports are handled under Apple's privacy policy.

7. Sharing with Third Parties

Because no information is collected, there is nothing to share with third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

The app collects no personal information, so it is safe to use at any age. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Deleting Your Data

  • Individual items and history entries can be deleted in the app at any time.
  • To remove all data, delete the app from your device.
  • Because no data is held by the developer, there is no deletion request process to follow.

10. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date revised. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.

11. Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, please contact:
